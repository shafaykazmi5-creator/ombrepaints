Artisan Protocol

The Wood Dyer™ 4-Step Application Routine

Professional carpentry instructions for unmatched sheen and protection.

01

Sand & Clean

Sand along wood grain with 120-grit, ending with 240-grit. Remove fine sawdust using WD Wood Thinner.

02

Lock Pores

Apply 1-2 coats of WD Wood Sealer diluted 15% with WD Thinner. Dry 1 hour, sand with 320-grit paper.

03

Dye & Tone

Wipe or spray WD Wood Colour. Allow 3 to 5 minutes for cellular penetration, then wipe excess with a clean rag.

04

Shield Topcoat

Apply 2 final coats of WD Clear Varnish, WD PU Varnish (floors/tables), or WD Lacquer.

100% Pure Acrylic Formulation

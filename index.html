Artisan Protocol

The Wood Dyer™ 4-Step Application Routine

Professional carpentry instructions for unmatched sheen and protection.

01

Sand & Clean

Sand along wood grain with 120-grit, ending with 240-grit. Remove fine sawdust using Wood Thinner.

02

Lock Pores

Apply 1-2 coats of Wood Sealer diluted 15% with Wood Thinner. Dry 1 hour, sand with 320-grit paper.

03

Dye & Tone

Wipe or spray Wood Colour. Allow 3 to 5 minutes for cellular penetration, then wipe excess with a clean rag.

04

Shield Topcoat

Apply 2 final coats of Synthetic Clear Varnish, Polyurethane (PU) Varnish (floors/tables), or Wood Lacquer.

Heritage & Quality

Ombré Paints • Sequence of Colors

Engineered to bridge architectural durability with rich artistic hues. Made with 100% acrylic polymeric binders and fine lightfast pigments.

100% Pure Acrylic Formulation

Unlike cheap chalk fillers that deteriorate and wash away during monsoon seasons, Ombré Paints uses pure acrylic resins. This gives our films high flexibility, expanding and contracting with wall temperature swings to prevent micro-cracks.

Under our dedicated division, Wood Dyer, we engineered specialty synthetic and polyurethane wood solutions. Our clear varnishes, sanding sealers, and penetrating dyes allow timber to breathe while shielding sensitive cellulose fibers from destructive moisture and rot.
